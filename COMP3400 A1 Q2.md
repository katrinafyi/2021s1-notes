$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname*{True} &= \lambda x. \lambda y.x \\ 
\operatorname*{False}& = \lambda x. \lambda y.y \\ 
\operatorname*{AND} &=\lambda p.\lambda q.((pq)p) \\ 
\operatorname*{OR} &=\lambda p.\lambda q.((pp)q) \\ 
\end{aligned}
$$
We will reduce $\operatorname*{OR} \operatorname*{True}$.
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname*{OR} \operatorname*{True} 
&= (\lambda p.\lambda q.(ppq))(\lambda x.\lambda y.x) && \text{by definition} \\ 
&\to (\lambda q.(ppq))[p := \lambda x.\lambda y.x] && \text{beta reduction} \\ 
&= \lambda q.(\lambda x.\lambda y.x)(\lambda x.\lambda y.x)q && \text{binding} \\ 
&\to \lambda q.(\lambda x'.\lambda y'.x')(\lambda x.\lambda y.x)q && \text{alpha conversion} \\ 
&\to \lambda q.(\lambda y'.x')[x':=\lambda x.\lambda y.x]q && \text{beta reduction} \\ 
&= \lambda q.(\lambda y'.(\lambda x.\lambda y.x))q && \text{bind} \\ 
&\to \lambda q. (\lambda x.\lambda y.x)[y':=q] && \text{beta reduction} \\ 
&= \lambda q. (\lambda x.\lambda y.x) && \text{bind (nothing)} \\ 
&= \lambda q. \operatorname*{True}
\end{aligned}
$$
Here, we can see that $\operatorname*{OR} \operatorname*{True}$ reduces to the constant function returning $\operatorname*{True}$, so the expression is a tautology.
